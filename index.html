About Feelndshare

Counselling that starts with listening

Feelndshare, led by Dr. Kabeer A, Consultant Psychologist & Relationship Coach, was founded on a simple belief: every feeling deserves space, and every person deserves to be heard. We create a warm, non-judgemental environment where you can speak openly, understand yourself better and build practical tools for everyday life.

We draw on evidence-based approaches, including CBT, Transactional Analysis, solution-focused and person-centred therapy, and tailor each session to you. No labels, no pressure, just steady support at your pace.

Questions

Frequently asked

Is everything I share kept confidential?

Yes. What you share stays between you and your counsellor. The only exceptions are rare situations where someone's safety is at serious risk, and we'll always explain this to you first.

Do I need to have a "serious problem" to see a counsellor?

Not at all. Many people come simply because they feel overwhelmed, want to understand themselves better, or need someone neutral to talk to.

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on you. Some people feel clearer after 3–4 sessions; others prefer ongoing support. We'll review progress together regularly.

Can I attend online if I'm not in Palakkad?

Yes. We offer video and phone sessions, so you can talk from anywhere in India or abroad, wherever you feel safe.

Which languages do you offer?

Sessions are available in English and Malayalam.
